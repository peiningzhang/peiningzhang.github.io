This review surveys diffusion-model approaches for molecular generation, covering their mathematical foundations, chemical applications, benchmark datasets, and evaluation metrics.

It places particular emphasis on 3D molecular generation, current challenges, and promising directions for applying diffusion models to drug discovery.
