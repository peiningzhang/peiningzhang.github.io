This work studies whether the Sundial time-series foundation model can forecast Leaf Area Index (LAI) without task-specific tuning.

Using the HiQ dataset and multiple evaluation protocols, it compares Sundial with statistical baselines, a supervised LSTM, and different context-window lengths. The results show that zero-shot Sundial can outperform a fully trained LSTM when the input covers more than one or two seasonal cycles.
